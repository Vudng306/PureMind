# Riverside Public Library — Annual Report 2025

## Letter from the director

This report describes how the Riverside Public Library served the town in 2025. It was a year of steady growth: more people visited, more children joined the summer reading programme, and the new evening opening hours on Thursdays were well used. The library's budget and staffing figures for the year are set out in the financial section of this report.

## Visitors and lending

The library welcomed 212,000 visits in 2025, up from 187,000 in 2024. Members borrowed 154,000 items, of which 38,000 were e-books and audiobooks. The number of registered members grew to 9,400. The busiest month was July, during the summer reading programme.

---

## Programmes

The summer reading programme had 1,150 children taking part, the largest number since the programme began. The library ran 96 workshops for adults, including 24 sessions helping older residents use online public services. A new homework club met twice a week during the school year and had an average attendance of 31 pupils.

## Plans for 2026

In 2026 the library plans to extend Thursday evening opening to Tuesdays as well, to replace the public computers in the reading room, and to start a reading group for teenagers. The director thanks the volunteers who gave more than 3,000 hours of their time in 2025.
